Why Pool Leak Detection Katy TX Pools Need Special Testing

Pool leak detection in Katy TX is more complicated than it looks because Katy's clay soils cause pool shells to shift seasonally just as they do slab foundations, and because the region's heavy annual rainfall makes it genuinely difficult to tell whether a pool is losing water to evaporation, splash-out, or a structural breach.

The bucket test — your first confirmation step

Before dispatching a technician, we walk homeowners through the bucket test: fill a plastic bucket with pool water to match the pool's current waterline, place it on a pool step, and mark both water levels. After 24 hours, compare the drop inside the bucket against the drop in the pool. If the pool has lost significantly more water than the bucket, evaporation is not the cause — a leak is likely. This simple test eliminates unnecessary call-outs and gives our team a confirmed starting point.

Pressure testing Katy pool plumbing

Once a leak is suspected, our network of licensed pros use pressure testing on the pool's plumbing lines — isolating each return, skimmer, and main drain circuit, pressurising to a known value, and monitoring for pressure decay. Water sensing equipment is then deployed along pipe runs and around fittings to locate the precise loss point. In Katy backyards, the soil movement that stresses slab plumbing also works on pool return lines buried in the same clay, so fitting separations at directional changes are a common finding. Dye testing at suspected shell cracks completes the inspection where shell integrity is in question.
