Our role in a gas leak situation begins after the utility has made the scene safe. Local pros use combustible-gas detectors — handheld instruments calibrated to detect natural gas at concentrations well below the lower explosive limit — to trace the leak path along the line from the meter to each appliance connection. This is not a visual inspection: the instrument probe moves along pipe runs, fittings, and valve bodies, and an audible alarm indicates concentration gradient, letting our technician follow the leak to its exact source.

To detect a natural gas leak in buried yard lines, we apply tracer-gas technology: a non-toxic marker gas is introduced into the isolated line and a surface sensor sweeps the soil above it, identifying the escape point without excavation. Leak Detection for Pools in Keller Backyards
